## 5. Alendei positioning (hypothesis — unvalidated)

**Working hypothesis:** Alendei should be evaluated as a *strategic energy-development and ecosystem-integration partner*, not merely a solar EPC.

Candidate roles to be tested (none are asserted as existing, licensed or contracted capabilities):

- Opportunity identification
- Project development
- Technical solution architecture
- Renewable integration
- BESS integration
- Industrial / captive energy solutions
- IPP / BOOT / BOO structures
- Commercial structuring
- Partner coordination
- OEM / EPC integration
- Financing coordination
- Government interface
- Industrial interface

Validation occurs at Gate 7 and requires (a) Guinea-side evidence of need and (b) user-supplied evidence of Alendei capability.
