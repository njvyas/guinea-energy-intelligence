### 4.1 Technology neutrality (D-027)

Solar, wind, hydro, BESS, thermal, hybrid systems, grid reinforcement, regional trade, demand-side measures, mini-grids and any other technically/economically justified solution are assessed on evidence. No technology is promoted or de-emphasised before the evidence supports that conclusion. Coverage by workstream: `workstream_charters.md` (technology-option coverage table).

## 5. Alendei positioning (hypothesis — unvalidated)

**Working hypothesis:** Alendei should be evaluated as a *strategic energy-development and ecosystem-integration partner*, not merely a solar EPC.

Candidate roles to be tested (none are asserted as existing, licensed or contracted capabilities):

- Opportunity identification
- Project development
- Technical solution architecture
- Renewable integration
- BESS integration
- Industrial / captive energy solutions
- IPP / BOOT / BOO structures
- Commercial structuring
- Partner coordination
- OEM / EPC integration
- Financing coordination
- Government interface
- Industrial interface

Validation occurs at Gate 7 in WS-28, after the WS-27 country opportunity ratings are frozen, and requires (a) Guinea-side evidence of need from Phase A and WS-27 and (b) user-supplied evidence of Alendei capability. The hypothesis may not influence WS-27 rankings.

## 6. Out of scope until later gates

The following are explicitly **not decided** and must emerge from evidence:

best technology · best geography · best project · investment size · project economics · tariff · IRR · NPV · DSCR · land requirement · final Alendei opportunity · final partner · final OEM · final financing structure.

Additionally, per the Gate 1 owner review (D-026), the following are **not assumed** and must be investigated: the national utility's financial condition; any requirement for offshore escrow or specific FX structures; the relevance of any river-basin organisation; any universal GIS exclusion threshold; any generic alumina-refinery load; lower priority for wind; the opportunity-screening weights; mining B2B as the preferred commercial model.
